Here's a quick test of this particular implementation of Markdown.

These words are separated by 2 consecutive dashes: ante--post. The rendered separator is `U+2013: EN-DASH`.

These words are separated by 3 consecutive dashes: ante---post. The rendered separator is `U+2014: EM DASH`.

This is consistent with the use of the `--smart` option to [`cmark (1)`](https://man.archlinux.org/man/cmark.1.en).
